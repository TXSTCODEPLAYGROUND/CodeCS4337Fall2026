Idea: make source and target features look the same, so a classifier trained on source labels also works on target.

Examples: DANN (adversarial), DAN (MMD distance), Deep CORAL (match feature covariances)

Idea: let the model label the target data itself, trust only confident guesses, and retrain. Augmentation and confidence thresholds keep errors from compounding.

Examples: Mean Teacher, FixMatch, Noisy Student

Idea: no retraining, no labels. Cheap updates to normalization layers absorb shifts such as new cameras, blur or lighting.

Examples: AdaBN, TENT

Idea: train a small model to match a large model's output distribution, not just the hard label. Fits domain adaptation too: adapt a big teacher, then distill it to a small deployable student.

Examples: Hinton et al. 2015; Noisy Student uses it for self-training

Examples: Adapters, LoRA, BN-only tuning
