Transfer Learning

Reuse a pretrained network's features, replace its classifier head, then train in two stages on your own dataset.

Layer2

Layer4

Keep the body: its features are general.
Drop the 1000-class layer — don't stack a 1000→10 layer on it.

3

Add a new head
for your 10 classes

🔒 Body (frozen at first)
Conv + MaxPool + Layer1–4
Global AvgPool → 512
NEW: FC 512 → 10 (random init)
fastai's default head is larger:
ConcatPool (avg+max → 1024) → BN → Dropout → Linear 1024→512 → ReLU → BN → Dropout → Linear 512→10
4

Train in two stages

Stage 1 · Feature extraction

  • Body frozen, train only the head
  • Normal LR, e.g. 1e-3
  • Fast; random head can't damage pretrained features

Stage 2 · Fine-tuning

  • Unfreeze body, train all layers
  • Lower LR, e.g. 1e-5–1e-4
  • Discriminative LRs: smaller for early layers, larger near the head

PyTorch explicit control over freezing and trainable params
import torch
import torch.nn as nn
from torchvision import models
from torchvision.models import ResNet18_Weights

# 1. Load pretrained ResNet18 (normalize inputs with ImageNet mean/std)
model = models.resnet18(weights=ResNet18_Weights.DEFAULT)

# 2. Freeze the pretrained body
for p in model.parameters():
    p.requires_grad = False

# 3. Replace the head: 512 features -> 10 classes (new layer is trainable)
model.fc = nn.Linear(model.fc.in_features, 10)

# 4a. Stage 1: train only the head with a normal LR
optimizer = torch.optim.Adam(model.fc.parameters(), lr=1e-3)
# ... train a few epochs ...

# 4b. Stage 2: unfreeze everything, fine-tune with lower LRs
for p in model.parameters():
    p.requires_grad = True
body = [p for n, p in model.named_parameters() if not n.startswith("fc.")]
optimizer = torch.optim.Adam([
    {"params": body,                  "lr": 1e-5},
    {"params": model.fc.parameters(), "lr": 1e-4},
])
fastai high-level: head, freezing and LR schedule handled for you
from fastai.vision.all import *
from torchvision.models import resnet18, ResNet18_Weights

# Your data (fastai adds ImageNet normalization automatically)
dls = ImageDataLoaders.from_folder(path, valid_pct=0.2,
                                   item_tfms=Resize(224))

# Cuts off avgpool + fc, adds a new head sized to dls.c,
# and freezes the pretrained body
learn = vision_learner(
    dls,
    resnet18,
    weights=ResNet18_Weights.DEFAULT,
    metrics=[accuracy, F1Score(average="macro")]
)

# Stage 1: 1 epoch, body frozen, head trained at base_lr (2e-3)
# Stage 2: unfreeze, 5 epochs with discriminative LRs
#          from 1e-5 (early layers) up to 1e-3 (head)
learn.fine_tune(5, base_lr=2e-3)

# Note: the argument is base_lr, not lr
Why freeze the body first?The new head starts random, so its early gradients are large and noisy. Sent through the whole network, they would wreck the pretrained features.
Why not train everything from the start?Millions of parameters on a small dataset overfit and overwrite the general features. Fine-tune later, gently, with a low LR.
Why replace the head, not stack on it?The 1000 ImageNet scores are a task-specific bottleneck. Linear-on-linear adds no expressive power. Attach the new head to the 512-d features.
BatchNorm caveatSetting requires_grad=False doesn't stop BN running stats from updating in train() mode. Put frozen BN layers in eval() to fully freeze them.
